# Engineer Value Product

Identify one buyer, repeated job, present workaround, and observable improvement. Select the smallest deliverable that can demonstrate the improvement. Compare reports, reusable datasets, notebooks, templates, skills, MCP tools, and hosted apps on actual reuse and integration needs.

Read [the product contract](references/contract.md) when defining artifacts or evidence. Audit existing repositories, catalogs, offers, skills, and account connections before adding a product or platform. Distinguish source code, a passing local test, deployed software, and accepted customer work. Preserve paid/private assets and brand responsibilities.

Research primary public sources and authorized customer material. Record dated evidence, actual buyer language, alternative costs, reuse rights, and conflicting findings. Label inferred or synthetic examples. A vendor testimonial or model-generated persona cannot establish willingness to pay. When customer evidence is absent, state the uncertainty and continue with a prototype that helps test it.

Define value before implementation: input, useful output, baseline, acceptance method, error cost, and maintenance owner. Research products require traceable citations, reproducibility, uncertainty and reuse rights. Software requires a working core path, failure behavior, portability and cost/latency evidence. Skills require a realistic task evaluation. Measure successful user work.

Deliver a product record with buyer/job, evidence ledger, versioned artifacts, acceptance criteria, offer hypothesis, fulfillment/support, owner-time economics and a falsifiable experiment. Reference an existing IncomeSystem or IncomeAsset rather than inventing a competing identity or silently changing its schema.

Advance to funnel design when the deliverable and its limits can be shown. A validation page may precede a finished product when status is explicit. Draft prices freely; make binding commitments only under the applicable authorization.
