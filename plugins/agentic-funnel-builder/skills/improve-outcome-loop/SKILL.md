# Improve Outcome Loop

Read [the metric contract](references/contract.md). Establish cohort, currency, window, denominator, event identity and attribution first. Preserve unknowns; missing data is not zero.

Trace eligible visitors through qualified intent, purchase, delivery, first useful result, acceptance and retained use. Repair product or fulfillment defects before adding acquisition. Aggregate customer/tenant information only with permission.

Reconcile paid, earned, refunded and adjusted events with platform/model/tool costs and human time. Distinguish gross sales, collected cash, earned revenue, contribution before/after acquisition and fixed overhead. Convert currencies explicitly.

Select one change that retires the largest uncertainty. Predeclare hypothesis, randomization unit where relevant, primary outcome, minimum useful effect, sample/time requirement, guardrails and stopping rule. Small cohorts support interviews and debugging, not winner claims. Do not repeatedly peek at ordinary fixed-horizon significance tests.

Preserve the current deployment until evidence and applicable authorization support a change. Deliver retain/repair/expand/pause/retire with evidence, economics, customer consequences, owner and next observation. Update product and skill evals from real failures without universalizing one customer's circumstances.
