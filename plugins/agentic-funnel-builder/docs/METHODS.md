## Principles and original extensions

- Treat Hook–Story–Offer as a useful message shape, then add demonstrable product utility and delivery evidence.
- Tie a value ladder to growing customer capability. Do not manufacture extra purchases to make the chart look complete.
- Use an actual founder learning experience rather than invented epiphanies or customer stories.
- Build a partner/audience map before buying traffic; use permissioned outreach and useful public contributions.
- Connect acquisition to activation and retained usefulness. More conversion can worsen the business when delivery and refunds deteriorate.
- Make ongoing maintenance, support and human time visible in the economics.

Dream 100 has broader provenance including Chet Holmes. Other teaching patterns must not be presented as uniquely invented by one author without source support.

## ClickFunnels: observed product and public funnel

The public DotCom book flow offers a physical-book acquisition route with optional audio and training add-ons. Our adaptation is a useful digital sample followed by optional implementation help. Digital delivery does not need a fictional shipping charge.

ClickFunnels offers funnels, websites, checkout, courses/community, email/CRM, testing and AI features. Current listed monthly plans are $97, $197 and $297; the pricing matrix places full API/webhooks at Scale and above. Recheck exact account and plan terms before procurement.

Its MCP beta connects through OAuth at https://agents.myclickfunnels.com/mcp and supports account reads/writes. The official document calls it a technical preview. This creates a buy/integrate path for execution, not a reason to build another vendor API wrapper.
